## Ingredients

- 600 g boneless chicken thighs, cut into strips
- 3 tbsp olive oil
- 2 tsp ground cumin
- 2 tsp smoked paprika
- 1 tsp ground coriander
- ½ tsp ground cinnamon
- 1 tsp salt
- 3 garlic cloves, grated (2 for the chicken, 1 for the yoghurt)
- 1 lemon, juiced
- 1 cauliflower (about 600 g), cut into florets
- 1 red pepper, sliced
- 2 red onions (1 cut into wedges, 1 thinly sliced for pickling)
- 60 ml white wine vinegar or apple cider vinegar
- 1 tsp sugar
- 200 g thick yoghurt (Greek or Turkish style)
- 1 cucumber, diced
- 4 flatbreads, to serve

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 220 °C (200 °C fan).
2. Mix 2 tbsp oil, the cumin, paprika, ground coriander, cinnamon, ½ tsp salt, 2 garlic cloves and half the lemon juice. Toss the chicken in it.
3. Spread the cauliflower, pepper and onion wedges on a large tray, toss with the remaining oil and a pinch of salt, and roast for 10 minutes.
4. Add the chicken to the tray in one layer and roast for 20–25 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the edges are charred.
5. Meanwhile, stir the last garlic clove, the remaining lemon juice and a pinch of salt into the yoghurt.
6. Warm the flatbreads. Serve with the chicken and vegetables, cucumber, garlic yoghurt and pickled onions.

## Make ahead

- Quick-pickled red onions: thinly slice 1 red onion and mix with the vinegar, sugar and ½ tsp salt in a jar.
- Marinate the chicken: do step 2 and keep the chicken covered in the fridge.
- Garlic yoghurt: do step 5.

## Notes

For lunch boxes, pack chicken, vegetables and yoghurt separately from the flatbread.
