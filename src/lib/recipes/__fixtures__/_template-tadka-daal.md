## Ingredients

- 250 g red lentils, rinsed
- 1 l water
- 1 tsp ground turmeric
- 1 tsp salt
- 2 tbsp oil or ghee
- 1 tsp cumin seeds
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 3 garlic cloves, finely chopped
- 20 g fresh ginger, grated
- 1 green chilli, sliced (optional)
- 2 tomatoes, chopped
- 1 tsp garam masala
- 100 g baby spinach
- ½ lemon, juiced
- 15 g fresh coriander, chopped
- 300 g basmati rice, to serve

## Instructions

1. Put the lentils, water and turmeric in a pot. Bring to the boil, skim off the foam, then simmer partly covered for about 20 minutes until the lentils are soft, stirring now and then. Stir in the salt.
2. Meanwhile, cook the rice according to the packet.
3. Heat the oil or ghee in a frying pan over medium heat. Add the cumin seeds and let them sizzle for a few seconds, then add the onion and cook for about 8 minutes until golden.
4. Add the garlic, ginger and chilli and cook for 1 minute. Add the tomatoes and garam masala and cook for about 5 minutes until jammy.
5. Stir this tadka into the lentils, add the spinach and let it wilt. Add a splash of water if the daal is too thick.
6. Finish with the lemon juice and coriander. Serve with the rice.

## Make ahead

- Chop aromatics: finely chop the onion, garlic, ginger and chilli.
- Cook the daal base: do step 1 only. On the night, make the tadka (steps 3–4), stir it in and add the spinach.

## Notes

The daal base (lentils only, without spinach) is the part that freezes; add spinach and coriander when reheating.
