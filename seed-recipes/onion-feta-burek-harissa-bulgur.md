## Ingredients

### For the bulgur salad
- 85 g bulgur
- 195 g cooked chickpeas, drained
- 1 ½ tsp harissa spice
- 1 cucumber
- ½ pomegranate
- 10 g fresh mint and coriander

### For the burek
- 1 red onion, thinly sliced
- 100 g feta
- 10 g fresh flat-leaf parsley
- 2 large tortillas
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tbsp butter

### For the garlic yoghurt
- 75 g thick yoghurt (Greek or Turkish style)
- ½ garlic clove
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Bring 250 ml water to the boil. Stir in the bulgur, drained chickpeas, harissa and ½ tsp salt, then cover and simmer on medium-low heat for 9–10 minutes until the bulgur is just tender.
2. Fry the onion in ½ tbsp oil and the butter over medium heat for 4–5 minutes until soft, then set aside. Chop the cucumber and finely chop the mint and coriander.
3. Take the seeds out of the pomegranate half (break it apart in a bowl of water so the white pith floats off). Mix the seeds with the cucumber and herbs.
4. Crumble the feta and chop the parsley. Cut the tortillas into pieces, lay feta, onion and parsley down the middle and fold the edges in to make parcels. Fry in the rest of the oil over medium-high heat, folded side down first, for 2–3 minutes per side until golden and the cheese has melted.
5. Crush the garlic and stir it into the yoghurt with a pinch of salt and pepper. Stir the cucumber and pomegranate mix into the bulgur.
6. Serve the bulgur salad with the bureks and a spoonful of garlic yoghurt.

## Make ahead

- Harissa bulgur: cook the bulgur with the harissa and salt (step 1), cool and keep in the fridge. Stir in the chickpeas, cucumber and pomegranate just before serving.
- Deseed pomegranate: take the seeds out and keep them covered in the fridge.
- Garlic yoghurt: crush the garlic and stir it into the yoghurt with a pinch of salt and pepper.
