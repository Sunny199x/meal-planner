## Ingredients

- 180 g orzo
- 1 red onion, cut into wedges
- 1 red pepper, cut into chunks
- 2 tomatoes, cut into chunks
- 1 garlic clove
- 2 tbsp tomato paste
- 1 tsp sweet paprika, replaces HelloFresh's "Hello Sunrise" spice blend
- ½ tsp chilli flakes (optional)
- 25 g gruyère, grated
- 100 g soft goat cheese
- 20 g almonds
- 10 g fresh basil
- 2 tbsp olive oil
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 220 °C (200 °C fan). Toss the onion, pepper and tomatoes with 1 tbsp oil, ½ tsp salt and some pepper on a lined tray. Roast for 15–25 minutes until soft.
2. Crush or finely chop the garlic.
3. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pan over medium heat. Fry the garlic and paprika for 30 seconds, then add the orzo and tomato paste and fry for another minute.
4. Add 450 ml water and ½ tsp salt and bring to the boil. Cover, lower to medium heat and simmer for 12–14 minutes, stirring now and then, until the orzo is just tender.
5. Roughly chop the basil and almonds. Stir the gruyère and chilli flakes into the orzo.
6. Serve the orzo topped with the roasted vegetables, crumbled goat cheese, basil and almonds.

## Make ahead

- Roast Mediterranean vegetables: do step 1, cool and keep in the fridge; warm through before serving.
