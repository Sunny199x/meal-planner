## Ingredients

### For the meatballs
- 225 g beef mince
- 1 egg
- 25 g breadcrumbs
- 100 g feta
- 1 red onion
- 1 ½ tsp souvlaki spice mix
- ½ tbsp oil

### For the bulgur and salad
- 85 g bulgur
- 1 ½ tsp harissa spice
- 1 tomato
- 1 cucumber
- 200 g romaine lettuce
- 1 tbsp olive oil

### For the garlic yoghurt
- 75 g thick yoghurt (Greek or Turkish style)
- 1 garlic clove
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Bring 250 ml water to the boil. Add the bulgur, harissa and ¼ tsp salt, cover and simmer on medium-low heat for 9–10 minutes until tender.
2. Crumble the feta finely and coarsely grate the onion. Mix the mince with the onion, feta, egg, breadcrumbs, souvlaki spice, ½ tsp salt and some pepper. Shape into about 10 meatballs with wet hands.
3. Fry the meatballs in the oil over medium-high heat for 10–12 minutes, turning them, and lower the heat once browned. They're done when cooked through with no pink in the middle.
4. Chop the tomato, peel the cucumber into ribbons and chop the core, and roughly chop the lettuce. Toss with the olive oil and a pinch of salt and pepper.
5. Crush the garlic and stir it into the yoghurt with a pinch of salt and pepper.
6. Serve the meatballs with the bulgur and salad, with a spoonful of garlic yoghurt on top.

## Make ahead

- Harissa bulgur: do step 1, cool and keep in the fridge. Serve cold or warmed through.
- Mix the beef and feta meatball mince: do step 2 and keep it covered in the fridge.
- Garlic yoghurt: do step 5.
