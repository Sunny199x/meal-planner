## Ingredients

### For the meatballs
- 250 g pork mince
- 1 egg
- 25 g breadcrumbs
- 1 tbsp mustard
- 50 g kale, chopped

### For the soup
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 1 carrot, diced
- 200 g celeriac, peeled and diced
- 300 g potatoes, peeled and diced
- 2 tsp chicken stock concentrate
- 25 ml soy sauce
- ½ tbsp oil

### For the gremolata
- 10 g fresh flat-leaf parsley
- 1 garlic clove
- 1 tbsp olive oil
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat and fry the kale for about 1 minute. Take it out and set half aside for the end.
2. In a bowl, mix the mince with the egg, breadcrumbs, mustard, the other half of the kale, ½ tsp salt and some pepper.
3. Finely chop the onion. Dice the carrot (no need to peel it), and peel and dice the potatoes and celeriac into roughly 1 cm cubes.
4. Fry the vegetables in the same pot for 1–2 minutes. Add 800 ml water, the stock concentrate and the soy sauce, and bring to the boil.
5. With wet hands, roll the mince into about 10 small meatballs and drop them in one at a time. Simmer on medium heat for 6–8 minutes, until the meatballs are cooked through with no pink in the middle.
6. For the gremolata, finely chop the parsley and garlic and mix with the olive oil, a pinch of salt and pepper.
7. Stir the reserved kale into the soup. Serve in bowls with the gremolata spooned on top.

## Make ahead

- Chop aromatics: finely chop the onion.
- Mix the pork meatball mince: do step 2 and keep it covered in the fridge.
