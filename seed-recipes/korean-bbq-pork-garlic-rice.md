## Ingredients

- 150 g jasmine rice
- 1 garlic clove
- 250 g thin pork loin chops
- 2 tsp BBQ spice mix
- 75 g Korean BBQ sauce
- 1 cucumber
- 1 carrot
- 1 lime
- 10 g sesame seeds
- ½ tsp sugar
- 200 g romaine lettuce
- 1 spring onion
- ½ tsp chilli flakes (optional)
- 1 ½ tbsp oil
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Crush the garlic and fry it in ½ tbsp oil in a pot over medium heat for a few seconds. Add 300 ml water and bring to the boil, then stir in the rice and ¼ tsp salt. Cover and simmer on medium-low heat for 12 minutes, then leave off the heat, covered, for 10 minutes.
2. Cut the cucumber into thin sticks. Peel the carrot into ribbons and chop the core. Mix a good squeeze of lime juice with the sugar and ¼ tsp salt, and toss the cucumber, carrot and sesame seeds in it.
3. Separate the lettuce leaves and slice the spring onion.
4. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a large pan over high heat. Season the pork with the BBQ spice, salt and pepper and fry for 1–2 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.
5. Mix the BBQ sauce with the chilli flakes and a pinch of salt. Slice the pork.
6. Serve the rice, pork, pickled vegetables and lettuce, topped with spring onion, with the sauce on the side. The lettuce leaves work as wraps.

## Make ahead

- Cook rice: cook the rice, cool it quickly and keep it in the fridge. Reheat until piping hot.
- Lime-pickled carrot and cucumber: cut the carrot and cucumber and toss with lime juice, sugar and salt. Add the sesame seeds just before serving.
