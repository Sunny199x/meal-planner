## Ingredients

- 2 sweet potatoes (about 700 g), cut into 2 cm cubes
- 1 tbsp oil
- 1 tsp ground cumin
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- ½ tsp chilli flakes (optional)
- 2 cans black beans (400 g each), drained and rinsed
- 1 garlic clove, grated
- ½ red onion, thinly sliced
- 30 ml white wine vinegar or apple cider vinegar
- 150 g thick yoghurt (Greek or Turkish style)
- 1 lime, zested and juiced
- 100 g feta, crumbled
- 15 g fresh coriander, chopped
- 1 avocado, sliced (optional)
- 8 small tortillas
- salt, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 220 °C (200 °C fan). Toss the sweet potato with the oil, cumin, paprika, chilli flakes and a pinch of salt. Roast for about 25 minutes, turning once, until soft and browned.
2. Warm the black beans in a pan with the garlic and a splash of water. Lightly mash about a third of them and season with salt.
3. Mix the yoghurt with the lime zest, half the lime juice and a pinch of salt.
4. Warm the tortillas. Fill with beans, sweet potato, feta, pickled onion, coriander, avocado and lime yoghurt. Squeeze over the remaining lime juice.

## Make ahead

- Quick-pickled red onions: thinly slice ½ red onion and mix with the vinegar and a pinch of salt in a jar.
- Roast the sweet potato: do step 1, cool, and keep in the fridge; reheat in the oven or a pan on the night.

## Notes

For lunch, pack the filling as a bowl (with rice if there is some) instead of tacos.
