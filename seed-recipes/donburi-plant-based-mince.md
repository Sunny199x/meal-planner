## Ingredients

- 150 g jasmine rice
- 1 tbsp butter
- 180 g plant-based mince
- 1 onion, finely chopped
- 75 g wok sauce
- 1 pak choi
- ½ kohlrabi
- 1 carrot
- 50 g sesame mayonnaise
- ½ garlic clove
- ¼ tsp chilli flakes (optional)
- 1 tbsp oil
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Bring 300 ml water to the boil, add the rice and ½ tsp salt, cover and simmer on medium-low heat for about 12 minutes. Take off the heat and leave covered for 10 minutes without lifting the lid.
2. Crush the garlic. Peel the carrot into ribbons, peel the kohlrabi and cut it into thin sticks, and finely chop the onion.
3. Heat the oil in a large pan over medium-high heat. Fry the mince and onion with ¼ tsp salt and some pepper for 4–5 minutes until lightly browned.
4. Whisk the sesame mayo with 2 tbsp water, the garlic and a pinch of salt and pepper. Toss the kohlrabi and carrot in it just before serving. Roughly chop the pak choi.
5. Add the wok sauce and 2 tbsp water to the mince and stir for about 30 seconds until coated. Stir the pak choi and butter through the hot rice.
6. Serve the rice topped with the slaw and mince, with chilli flakes if you like.

## Make ahead

- Cook rice: cook the rice, cool it quickly and keep it in the fridge. Reheat until piping hot.
- Sesame slaw: cut the kohlrabi and carrot and mix the dressing; keep them separate and combine just before serving.
