## Ingredients

- 300 g chicken breast strips
- 2 tsp taco seasoning, replaces HelloFresh's "Tex-Mex" spice blend
- 450 g sweet potato
- 1 can sweetcorn (about 340 g), drained
- 1 spring onion
- 75 g feta
- ½ mango
- ½ lime
- 10 g fresh coriander
- ¼ tsp chilli flakes (optional)
- 150 g pointed cabbage, shredded
- 75 ml sour cream
- 1 tsp smoked paprika
- 25 g mayonnaise
- 8 mini tortillas
- 2 tbsp oil
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 240 °C (220 °C fan). Cut the sweet potato into wedges, toss with 1 tbsp oil, salt and pepper, and roast on a lined tray for 20–30 minutes until soft.
2. Dice the mango and chop the coriander. Mix the mango, coriander, chilli flakes, a squeeze of lime, a pinch of salt and the cabbage in a large bowl. In a small bowl, mix the sour cream with the smoked paprika and a pinch of salt.
3. Fry the drained corn in a pan over medium heat for 4–5 minutes until lightly browned. Slice the spring onion and mix it with the corn and a pinch of salt and pepper.
4. In the same pan, fry the chicken in the rest of the oil with the taco seasoning and a pinch of salt for 4–5 minutes until browned and cooked through.
5. Warm the tortillas in the microwave for 30–60 seconds, or wrapped in foil in the oven for 4–5 minutes.
6. Fill the tortillas with the slaw, chicken and smoky sour cream. Crumble the feta over the corn and serve with the sweet potato and mayonnaise.

## Make ahead

- Roast sweet potato: do step 1, cool and keep in the fridge. Reheat in the oven.
