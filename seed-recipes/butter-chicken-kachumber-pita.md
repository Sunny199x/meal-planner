## Ingredients

### For the curry
- 300 g chicken breast fillets
- 30 g tikka masala paste
- 35 g tomato paste
- 1 tsp mild curry powder, replaces HelloFresh's "Milda Mahal" spice blend
- 150 ml cooking cream
- 1 tsp chicken stock powder
- 2 tbsp butter

### For the rice
- 150 g basmati rice
- 15 g desiccated coconut

### For the salad and pita
- ½ red onion
- 1 lime
- 1 tsp sugar
- ½ pomegranate
- ½ cucumber
- 125 g cherry tomatoes
- 10 g fresh mint
- 2 pita breads
- 3 tbsp oil
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 220 °C (200 °C fan). Toast the coconut in ½ tbsp oil in a pot over medium heat for about 30 seconds. Add 300 ml water, the rice and ½ tsp salt, bring to the boil, then cover and simmer on medium-low heat for 10 minutes. Leave off the heat, covered, for 10 minutes.
2. Squeeze the lime into a small bowl with ½ tsp sugar and a pinch of salt. Thinly slice the onion and stir it in.
3. Take the seeds out of the pomegranate. Chop the cucumber and halve the tomatoes, and mix them with the pomegranate seeds. Finely chop the mint and mix it with 1 tbsp oil and a pinch of salt and pepper.
4. Thinly slice the chicken and fry it in 1 tbsp oil over medium-high heat for 3–4 minutes until browned. Add the tikka paste, tomato paste, curry powder, ½ tsp sugar and the butter and fry for 30 seconds. Add the stock powder, ½ tsp salt, the cream and 100 ml water and simmer for 1–2 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
5. Brush the pitas with the mint oil and bake on a lined tray for 2–3 minutes until crisp. Toss the salad with the pickled onion and its juice, ½ tbsp oil and a pinch of salt.
6. Serve the rice and curry with the salad and pita.

## Make ahead

- Cook rice: cook the rice, cool it quickly and keep it in the fridge. Reheat until piping hot.
- Quick-pickled red onions: do step 2.
- Deseed pomegranate: take the seeds out and keep them covered in the fridge.
