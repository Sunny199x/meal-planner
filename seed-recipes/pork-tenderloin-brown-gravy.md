## Ingredients

- 300 g pork tenderloin
- 500 g potatoes
- 100 g ready-made brown gravy
- 150 g red cabbage, shredded
- 20 g dried cranberries
- 20 g honey mustard dressing
- 10 g fresh flat-leaf parsley
- 2 tbsp oil
- 2 tbsp butter
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 240 °C (220 °C fan) and put a pot of salted water on to boil. Brown the pork in 1 tbsp hot oil over high heat for about 2 minutes per side and season with salt. Roast on a tray for 12–15 minutes until no longer pink in the middle, then rest under foil.
2. Cut the potatoes (no need to peel) into 2 cm pieces and boil for 11–13 minutes until tender. Drain, season and keep covered.
3. Finely chop the parsley.
4. Mix the red cabbage with the cranberries, dressing, 1 tbsp oil and a pinch of salt and pepper.
5. Put the unwashed meat pan on medium heat and warm the gravy with 2 tbsp water, a pinch of salt and 1 tbsp butter. Slice the pork about 2 cm thick. Toss the potatoes with the rest of the butter, the parsley and a little salt.
6. Serve the pork, potatoes and salad with the gravy poured over the pork.

## Make ahead

- Red cabbage salad: do step 4 and keep it covered in the fridge.
