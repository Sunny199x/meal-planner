## Ingredients

- 200 g vegan kebab strips
- 1 tsp taco seasoning, replaces HelloFresh's "Panamat" spice blend
- 500 g potatoes
- 1 ½ tsp souvlaki spice mix
- 75 ml sour cream
- 25 g mayonnaise
- ½ garlic clove
- 1 small lettuce
- 1 tomato
- 1 cucumber
- 40 g radishes
- 1 onion, thinly sliced
- 100 g feta
- 2 tbsp oil
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 240 °C (220 °C fan). Cut the potatoes (no need to peel) into thin fries, toss with 1 tbsp oil, the souvlaki spice, ½ tsp salt and some pepper, and roast for 25–35 minutes until golden. Use two trays if they're crowded.
2. Crush the garlic and mix it with the sour cream and mayonnaise. Season with salt and pepper.
3. Finely shred the lettuce, chop the tomato and cucumber, thinly slice the radishes and crumble the feta.
4. About 10 minutes before the fries are ready, fry the vegan strips and onion with the taco seasoning, salt and pepper in a little oil over medium heat for 3–4 minutes until browned.
5. Just before serving, toss the lettuce, tomato, cucumber, radishes and feta with the rest of the oil and a pinch of salt and pepper.
6. Serve the fries topped with the salad and strips, with the garlic sauce drizzled over.

## Make ahead

- Cut potato fries: cut the potatoes into fries and keep them covered in cold water in the fridge. Dry them well before roasting.
- Garlic sauce: do step 2.
