## Ingredients

- 300 g sirloin or rump steak
- 500 g potatoes
- 1 sprig fresh rosemary
- 150 g broccolini
- 1 carrot
- 1 onion
- 2 garlic cloves
- 1 tsp coarsely ground black pepper
- 100 ml cooking cream
- ½ beef stock cube
- 2 tbsp oil
- salt, to taste

## Instructions

1. Take the steak out of the fridge and salt both sides. Heat the oven to 240 °C (220 °C fan). Cut the potatoes (no need to peel) into fries about 1 cm thick, toss with the chopped rosemary leaves, 1 tbsp oil, salt and pepper, and bake for 25–30 minutes until crisp.
2. Cut the carrot into sticks about 5 cm long and the onion into wedges. Crush the garlic.
3. Fry the broccolini, carrot and onion in a little oil with a splash of water over medium-high heat for 4–5 minutes until just tender. Add half the garlic for the last minute, season, and keep warm in a covered bowl.
4. Add a little more oil to the same pan. Sear the steak on high heat for 1 minute per side, then lower the heat and cook for 1–2 minutes per side for medium-rare (longer if you prefer). Rest it wrapped in foil.
5. About 5 minutes before the fries are ready, put the pan (with the meat juices) on medium-low heat. Fry the black pepper and the rest of the garlic for 20–30 seconds, then add a splash of water, the cream, the stock and a pinch of salt. Simmer for 1–2 minutes until slightly thickened and stir in the juices from the resting steak.
6. Slice the steak thinly and serve with the fries, vegetables and sauce.

## Make ahead

- Cut potato fries: cut the potatoes into fries and keep them covered in cold water in the fridge. Dry them well before roasting.
