## Ingredients

- 225 g beef mince
- 2 tsp taco seasoning, replaces HelloFresh's "Tex-Mex" spice blend
- 1 can crushed tomatoes (390 g)
- 1 can black beans (390 g), drained and rinsed
- ½ red chilli or jalapeño (optional)
- 1 tsp beef stock powder
- 75 ml sour cream
- 2 tsp sriracha
- 1 bag tortilla chips
- 1 tbsp oil
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Deseed and finely chop the chilli. Drain and rinse the beans.
2. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium-high heat. Brown the mince with the taco seasoning, ½ tsp salt and some pepper for 4–5 minutes until cooked through.
3. Add the tomatoes, chilli, beans, stock powder and 50 ml water. Simmer uncovered for 8–10 minutes until thick, then season to taste.
4. Mix the sour cream with the sriracha and a pinch of salt.
5. Serve the chili in bowls with the sriracha dressing and tortilla chips.

## Make ahead

- Cook the chili: do steps 1–3, cool quickly and keep in the fridge. Reheat until piping hot.
