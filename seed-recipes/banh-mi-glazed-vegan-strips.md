## Ingredients

- 200 g vegan kebab strips
- 1 tsp ground ginger, replaces HelloFresh's "Ginger Ninja" spice blend
- ½ tsp garlic powder
- 25 ml soy sauce
- 40 g sweet chilli sauce
- 300 g potatoes
- 10 g sesame seeds
- 2 small baguettes
- 1 carrot
- 1 cucumber
- 1 lime
- 10 g fresh mint
- 50 g sriracha mayonnaise
- 1 ½ tbsp oil
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 240 °C (220 °C fan). Cut the potatoes (no need to peel) into thin fries, toss with ½ tbsp oil, ¼ tsp salt and some pepper, and roast for 25–35 minutes until golden. Scatter the sesame seeds over at the end.
2. Peel the carrot and cucumber into ribbons and finely chop the carrot core. Finely chop the mint and halve the lime.
3. Toss the carrot and cucumber with the mint, a squeeze of lime and a pinch of salt.
4. When the fries have about 8 minutes left, put the baguettes straight on the oven rack to crisp up.
5. Heat 1 tbsp oil in a pan over medium heat and fry the vegan strips with the ginger and garlic powder for 3–4 minutes until browned. Add the soy sauce and sweet chilli sauce and toss for 1–2 minutes until glazed.
6. Split the baguettes, spread with some sriracha mayo and fill with the strips and salad. Serve with the fries and the rest of the mayo and salad.

## Make ahead

- Cut potato fries: cut the potatoes into fries and keep them covered in cold water in the fridge. Dry them well before roasting.
- Lime-pickled carrot and cucumber: peel the carrot and cucumber into ribbons and toss with lime juice and a pinch of salt. Add the mint just before serving.
