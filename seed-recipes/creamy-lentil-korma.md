## Ingredients

- 150 g basmati rice
- 15 g desiccated coconut
- 1 pack cooked green lentils (about 380 g), drained
- 20 g yellow curry paste
- 1 tsp mild curry powder, replaces HelloFresh's "Milda Mahal" spice blend
- 1 tsp vegetable stock powder
- 150 ml cooking cream
- 60 g baby spinach
- ½ pomegranate
- ½ red chilli (optional)
- 20 g salted peanuts
- 50 g apricot chutney
- 2 tbsp butter
- salt, to taste

## Instructions

1. Bring 300 ml water to the boil, add the rice and ½ tsp salt, cover and simmer on medium-low heat for about 10 minutes. Take off the heat and leave covered for 10 minutes. Stir in the coconut just before serving.
2. Rinse the spinach and drain the lentils. Take the seeds out of the pomegranate. Deseed and finely chop the chilli.
3. Melt the butter in a large frying pan over medium heat and fry the curry powder and curry paste for about 1 minute until fragrant.
4. Add the lentils, stock powder, ½ tsp salt and the cream and simmer for 3–4 minutes until slightly thickened.
5. Stir in the spinach just before serving and let it wilt.
6. Serve the korma with the rice, topped with pomegranate seeds, chilli, peanuts and a spoonful of chutney.

## Make ahead

- Cook rice: cook the rice, cool it quickly and keep it in the fridge. Reheat until piping hot, then stir in the coconut.
- Deseed pomegranate: take the seeds out and keep them covered in the fridge.

## Notes

Original title: Krämig linskorma. Quantities are HelloFresh's 2-portion amounts.

HelloFresh supplies "1 pack" of green lentils; about 380 g is our estimate. Some Thai-style yellow curry pastes contain shrimp paste, so check the label. Mango chutney works instead of apricot. Method rewritten in our own words.
