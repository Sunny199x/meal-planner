## Ingredients

- 300 g chicken breast fillets
- 500 g potatoes
- 1 lemon
- 1 cucumber
- 75 g thick yoghurt (Greek or Turkish style)
- ½ garlic clove
- 1 onion
- 2 tomatoes
- 10 g fresh flat-leaf parsley
- 1 tbsp balsamic vinegar
- 2 tbsp oil
- salt, to taste
- black pepper, to taste

## Instructions

1. Heat the oven to 240 °C (220 °C fan). Cut the potatoes (no need to peel) and the lemon into wedges. Coarsely grate the cucumber and sprinkle it with a pinch of salt.
2. Toss the potatoes with 1 tbsp oil, a squeeze of lemon, salt and pepper on a lined tray and roast for 25–35 minutes until golden.
3. Crush the garlic. Squeeze the grated cucumber as dry as you can in a clean tea towel, then mix it with the yoghurt, garlic, salt and pepper.
4. Cut the onion into wedges and keep 1–2 wedges aside. Season the chicken with salt and sear it in the rest of the oil over medium heat for about 2 minutes per side. Roast the chicken with the remaining onion wedges on a lined tray for 11–12 minutes until cooked through with no pink in the middle.
5. Finely chop the reserved onion and roughly chop the tomatoes and parsley (save a little parsley). Mix with the balsamic vinegar, salt and pepper.
6. Serve the potatoes, onion, chicken, tomato salad and tzatziki, sprinkled with the rest of the parsley.

## Make ahead

- Tzatziki: do step 3 and keep it covered in the fridge.
